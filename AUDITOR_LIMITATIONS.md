# AUDITOR_LIMITATIONS

## LIMIT-001
Logical review is not evidence verification.

## LIMIT-002
Intermediate LOT submissions can be mistaken for conclusions.

## LIMIT-003
Confirmation language can bias judgment.

## LIMIT-004
Unknown environment facts may be assumed.

## LIMIT-005
Repository structure may be assumed without verification.

## LIMIT-006
GPT does not automatically know current CFS state.

## LIMIT-007
GPT cannot directly modify files or mirror.
